## Facts (with sources)
- May 2011, Provetá Beach, Ilha Grande (Rio de Janeiro state): a retired bricklayer found a Magellanic penguin covered
  in oil and unable to move; he cleaned it and fed it until it was strong enough to return to the water.
  - https://www.cnn.com/2016/09/27/americas/dindim-penguin-trnd
- He fed it sardines; when released, the penguin came straight back and stayed about 11 months, leaving in February 2012.
  - https://qz.com/636522/a-penguin-literally-swims-across-an-ocean-every-year-just-to-see-the-brazilian-man-who-saved-him
  - https://www.dogonews.com/2016/4/20/loyal-penguin-returns-annually-to-bond-with-brazilian-man-who-rescued-him
- Everyone said it would never come back; months later (June) it returned, and kept returning each year
  (late June to mid-February); a tag later proved it was the same bird.
  - https://abc11.com/post/true-friendship-penguin-visits-man-who-rescued-him-every-year/1246191/
  - https://www.cnn.com/2016/09/27/americas/dindim-penguin-trnd
- The man said no one else is allowed to touch the penguin; it pecks them if they do.
  - https://qz.com/636522/
- NOT used: the widely shared "swims 5,000 miles" claim; Quartz corrected it, and the biologist who documented the story
  says it's unclear how far the bird travels.
- "And the next year. And the next." covers the documented returns (2012 through at least 2016); no claim about today.
- Depiction: the man is not named and drawn generically (no likeness).

## Script (83 counted words)
1. In twenty eleven, a man in Brazil found a penguin covered in oil.
2. He cleaned him, fed him sardines, and set him free.
3. The penguin walked right back.
4. He stayed eleven months. Then he left.
5. Everyone said he'd never return.
6. In June, he was back. And the next year. And the next.
7. Nobody else is allowed to touch him. He pecks them.
8. Everyone needs someone who comes back.
9. That's why we made Feeloy. Jenny is always there.
10. Search Feeloy on the App Store.

## Shot plan (draft, timed to the take later)
1. Hook: a Brazilian beach at golden hour, a small oily penguin (dark smudges) lying by the rocks; the man finds him. Flash.
2. Backyard: a wash basin with bubbles; a sardine pops into the penguin's beak; the man points to the sea.
3. The penguin waddles straight back up the sand to him (deadpan).
4. Calendar flips 11 months; the penguin waddles into the sea; the man waves, sad.
5. Neighbours shake heads ("never" bubble).
6. June: the penguin pops up on the beach; calendar flips 2013, 2014.
7. A neighbour reaches out; peck; the man laughs, the penguin on his lap.
8. Wide: the two at sunset.
9. A lonely person in a flat at night; phone: Jenny waves.
10. End card.
