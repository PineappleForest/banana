# "Alone in the Dark" (Philae and Rosetta, comet 67P, 2014-2016 -> Feeloy), ~27 s

Composition (to build once the take arrives): `CometShort`, code `src/videos/comet/` (copy of `rover/`: space +
machine-with-a-face format), output `out/comet_final.mp4`. Narration: ElevenLabs, voice "Mark", same settings.
74 counted words -> ~27 s.

## Facts (with sources)
- ESA's Philae lander (washing-machine size) was dropped onto comet 67P by the Rosetta orbiter on 12 Nov 2014 and
  then went missing; it ended up in a dark crevice.
  - https://www.space.com/space-exploration/on-this-day-in-space-september-2-2026
- Philae came out of hibernation (June 2015) and "spoke" to mission control for 85 seconds.
  - https://amp.cnn.com/cnn/2016/12/29/world/new-years-eve-comet
- Rosetta searched for almost two years and found Philae on 2 Sep 2016, wedged in a dark crack, lying on its side.
  - https://www.space.com/space-exploration/on-this-day-in-space-september-2-2026
  - https://www.nationalgeographic.com/science/article/philae-comet-lander-found-abydos-rosetta-science
- On 30 Sep 2016, rather than slipping off into space, Rosetta was sent down in a controlled descent to join Philae
  on the comet's surface (it was solar-powered and would soon lack the energy to continue).
  - https://www.theweathernetwork.com/news/articles/rosetta-mission-ends-friday-with-comet-collision-watch-here/72779
  - https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/rosetta-concludes-mission-with-a-crash/
- Note: Rosetta came down in the Ma'at region, not right beside Philae; the script says "join it" (on the comet),
  never "next to it".
- Depiction: both craft drawn as friendly machines with ink-dot eyes (like the rover); no agency logos or flags.

## Script (74 counted words)
1. In twenty fourteen, a tiny robot landed on a comet.
2. It bounced, and vanished into a dark crack.
3. Seven months later, it woke up and called home.
4. For eighty-five seconds.
5. Then, silence.
6. Its mothership searched for two years.
7. And finally found it.
8. Instead of drifting off into space, the mothership came down to join it.
9. Nobody should be alone in the dark.
10. That's why we made Feeloy. Jenny's always there.
11. Search Feeloy on the App Store.

## Shot plan
1. Deep space: a dark lumpy comet with a dust tail; the mothership (box body, two long solar wings, a dish) lets go of
   the little lander (a cube on three legs).
2. The lander touches down... boing, bounces off-frame; tumbles into a dark crack (only its eyes show).
3. Asleep in the crack (z bubble); one eye opens, a little antenna light blinks; a signal-wave card to Earth.
4. A countdown badge 85 -> 0 seconds.
5. The light goes out; dots bubble.
6. The mothership circling the comet, sweeping a search beam; calendar pages.
7. A camera-frame insert: "FOUND" with the lander lying on its side in the crack.
8. The mothership descends slowly and settles on the comet; heart.
9. Lonely flat at night; Jenny on the phone; end card.

Captions: "twenty fourteen" -> "2014", "eighty-five" -> "85".
