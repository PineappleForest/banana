## Script (170 counted words)
1. In twenty sixteen, a grandmother in Arizona invited a total stranger to Thanksgiving. By accident.
2. She texted her grandson the time and place. But her grandson had changed his number.
3. The text landed on a seventeen-year-old. In the middle of class.
4. He asked, "Who is this?" She said, "Grandma."
5. He asked for a picture. She sent a selfie.
6. Definitely not his grandma.
7. So he sent one back, and asked the only question that mattered: "Can I still get a plate?"
8. She said, "Of course. That's what grandmas do. Feed everyone."
9. He showed up. So did the news crews. He could barely find a parking spot.
10. Then he came back the next year. And the next.
11. When her husband passed away, he was at her table.
12. Ten Thanksgivings later, they're still at it.
13. All because one text reached the wrong person, and the wrong person answered.
14. Not everyone gets a text like that.
15. That's why we made Feeloy. Text Jenny any time, and someone always answers.
16. Search Feeloy on the App Store.
