## Facts (with sources)
- Curiosity landed on Mars on August 5, 2012 (PDT); for the first anniversary (Aug 2013) NASA Goddard engineers used the
  Sample Analysis at Mars (SAM) instrument to "sing" Happy Birthday by vibrating it at different frequencies.
  - https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/11331/
  - https://nextgov.com/digital-government/2013/08/nasa-curiosity-cruising-one-year-after-mars-landing/212546
- "Over 200 million miles from Earth" at the time.
  - https://www.theregister.com/2013/08/06/curiositys_sam_sings_itself_happy_birthday_for_martian_anniversary/
- It only did it once: the rover's account later said it only hummed Happy Birthday to itself once, back in 2013; the
  SAM team lead said there is no scientific gain from the rover playing music.
  - https://www.nextgov.com/emerging-tech/2017/08/why-curiosity-rover-stopped-singing-happy-birthday-itself/140176/
- For its 10th birthday (Aug 5, 2022) robotic lawnmowers on Earth (a Husqvarna campaign, ~100,000 expected) sang Happy
  Birthday to Curiosity. The script says "an army of robot lawnmowers"; no number and no brand on screen.
  - https://earthsky.org/space/robots-to-robot-happy-birthday-to-you-curiosity/
- Depiction: the rover is drawn as our own generic six-wheeled rover (no NASA logos or mission marks); engineers generic.

## Script (81 counted words)
1. In twenty thirteen, a robot on Mars turned one year old.
2. So NASA made it sing "Happy Birthday." To itself.
3. By vibrating its science lab.
4. Over two hundred million miles from home.
5. It never sang again.
6. Singing, it turns out, has no scientific value.
7. But for its tenth birthday, an army of robot lawnmowers on Earth sang it back.
8. Nobody should have to sing to themselves.
9. That's why we made Feeloy. Jenny will always answer.
10. Search Feeloy on the App Store.

## Shot plan (draft, timed to the take later)
1. Hook: red Martian plain, a little six-wheeled rover alone; a "1" candle badge pops. Flash.
2. Mission control: an engineer types; a music-note bubble flies to a screen showing the rover.
3. CU: the rover's lab box shivers; music notes rise; nobody around.
4. Pull back: the rover tiny, Earth a blue dot in the sky (deep space feel).
5. Calendar flips 2014, 2015, 2016...; rover silent; "dots".
6. Engineer shrugs; a "SCIENCE" badge with music crossed out.
7. Earth: a green lawn at golden hour, a row of little robot mowers beeping music notes up to the sky.
8. Mars again: the rover's mast tilts up; heart.
9. A lonely person at night; phone: Jenny waves, replies.
10. End card.
