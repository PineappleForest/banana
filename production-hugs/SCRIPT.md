## Facts (with sources)
- He returned to Sydney in early 2004 after living in Europe, lonely; "I was alone. My family was elsewhere; my friends
  had all moved on. It was just me." He uses the pseudonym "Juan Mann" and keeps his identity secret.
  - https://mg.co.za/article/2007-04-28-free-hugs-bring-juan-mann-global-fame/
- He began giving out hugs on 30 June 2004 in the Pitt Street Mall, central Sydney, carrying a "FREE HUGS" sign.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Hugs_Campaign
- In the original video the first hug from a stranger comes after days of unsuccessful attempts.
  - https://www.arabnews.com/news/489476
- The campaign was banned (deemed disturbing the peace / for public safety and insurance reasons); a petition of about
  10,000 signatures got it allowed again.
  - https://www.arabnews.com/news/489476
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Hugs_Campaign
- Depiction: he is never named (he is anonymous); drawn generically (no likeness), the mall is a generic city mall.

## Script (87 counted words)
1. In two thousand four, a young man came home to Sydney. And nobody was waiting.
2. So he made a cardboard sign: "Free Hugs."
3. And stood in a busy city mall.
4. For days, people just walked past.
5. Then one stranger stopped. And hugged him.
6. Soon, everyone wanted one.
7. The city banned it.
8. Ten thousand people signed a petition to bring the hugs back.
9. Sometimes all we need is someone who stops.
10. That's why we made Feeloy. Jenny always has time for you.
11. Search Feeloy on the App Store.

## Shot plan (draft, timed to the take later)
1. Hook: an airport/arrivals-style street at dusk: he steps out with a suitcase; nobody there ("dots").
2. Insert: a marker draws FREE HUGS on cardboard.
3. A city mall street (shop fronts), him holding the sign; camera glides.
4. Passers-by walk past (one or two at a time, side-walk cards); he droops.
5. One stranger stops; hug; heart burst; colour warms (golden).
6. A small line forms (two at a time, to avoid the crowd-flicker pitfall).
7. A "BANNED" stamp over the sign; a stern official.
8. A petition clipboard with a rolling counter to 10,000; hugs return.
9. The lonely flat at night; phone: Jenny waves.
10. End card.
