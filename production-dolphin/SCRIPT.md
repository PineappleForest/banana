## Facts (with sources)
- A wild male bottlenose dolphin, Fungie, was first seen in Dingle harbour, County Kerry, Ireland, in 1983 and stayed
  there until he vanished in October 2020 (37 years). He sought out human company and swam close to tourist boats.
  - https://www.irishcentral.com/culture/bronze-statue-fungie-dolphin-dingle
  - https://news.yahoo.com/irelands-beloved-wild-dolphin-fungie-091900999.html
- Local boat tours offered money-back guarantees if he didn't appear.
  - https://www.irishcentral.com/culture/bronze-statue-fungie-dolphin-dingle
- Guinness World Records (2019): the longest-recorded solitary dolphin.
  - https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/592354-longest-recorded-solitary-dolphin
- In the 2020 lockdown, with tours stopped, a local tour-boat operator went out to keep him company (CNN, 2021).
  - https://abc17news.com/news/2021/10/17/a-dolphins-decades-long-stay-made-this-irish-town-famous-a-year-after-he-left-for-good-dingle-bids-farewell-to-fungie/
- Depiction: the dolphin is drawn generically (no name said); the boatman is unnamed and generic; no logos.

## Script (81 counted words)
1. In nineteen eighty-three, a wild dolphin swam into a tiny Irish harbor. Alone.
2. And he never left.
3. Every day, he swam beside the fishing boats.
4. Boat tours promised your money back if he didn't show up.
5. He almost always did.
6. For thirty-seven years.
7. When lockdown emptied the harbor, one boatman went out anyway, just to keep him company.
8. Everyone needs someone who shows up.
9. That's why we made Feeloy. Jenny shows up for you, every day.
10. Search Feeloy on the App Store.
